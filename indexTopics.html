Richat Structure "The Eye Of The Sahara"

The Eye of the Sahara, also known as the Richat Structure, is a massive 40–50 km wide circular geological formation formed as an uplifted geologic dome that was later eroded into concentric rings. Possibly the resting place of the lost city of Atlantis.
